The short version

Be 18 or over. Be who you say you are. Don't harass anyone. Don't post things you don't have the right to post. Meet strangers carefully.

Break those and your account goes. There is no warning system for harassment.

These terms are an agreement between you and Jammy LLC, a Washington limited liability company ("Jammy", "we"). By creating an account you accept them. If you don't, don't use Jammy.

1. Who can use Jammy

  • You must be 18 or over. Jammy has private messaging and location-based discovery between strangers, and we keep it to adults.
  • You must give an accurate date of birth. Lying about your age is grounds for a permanent ban.
  • One account per person. Don't create a new one to get around a ban.
  • If we have removed your account before, you may not come back under another name.

2. Zero tolerance for abusive content and behaviour

This is the part that matters most, and it is not negotiable. The following result in immediate removal, usually on a first report:

  • Harassment of any kind, including unwanted sexual messages, propositions, or continued contact after someone asks you to stop.
  • Slurs, hate speech, or targeting anyone over race, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability or nationality.
  • Threats of violence, intimidation, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves.
  • Sexual content, and any sexual content involving minors, which we also report to the authorities.
  • Impersonating another person, band or business.
  • Posting someone's private information, including their address.
  • Recording or sharing someone's audio or video without their knowledge.
  • Scams, fraudulent listings, fake auditions, or anything designed to take money from musicians.

There is no warning system for harassment. We would rather lose a user than keep someone who makes the app unsafe.

3. Jammy is not a dating app

It borrows a swipe because it is an efficient way to review profiles. That is the only thing it borrows. Using Jammy to pursue people romantically, when they came here to find a band, is a misuse of it and is treated as harassment if unwelcome.

4. Reporting, blocking and moderation

  • Every profile, post, comment and message can be reported from inside the app.
  • A person reads every report, and we aim to action them within 24 hours.
  • You can block anyone. Blocking is instant, silent, and removes them from your discovery, feed and messages.
  • We may remove content, suspend or permanently remove accounts, with or without notice, where we believe these terms have been broken.
  • We may contact law enforcement where there is a credible risk of harm.

5. Your content

  • You own everything you upload — your recordings, photos, video and writing. We do not claim ownership of your music.
  • You give us a limited licence to host, store and display your content inside Jammy so the app can function. It ends when you delete the content or your account.
  • Only upload what you have the right to. Don't post recordings of other people's songs you have no rights to, or photos you didn't take, or your band's recording without the band's agreement.
  • We may remove content that breaks these terms or that we are legally required to remove.

6. Meeting people in person

Jammy introduces you to strangers. We do not verify identity, run background checks, or vouch for anybody.

Meet in a public place or a staffed rehearsal space the first time. Tell someone where you are going. Don't go alone to a private address you have never been to. Trust your instincts and leave if something feels wrong.

You are responsible for your own safety when you choose to meet someone. Report anyone who makes you uncomfortable.

7. Bands, schedules and shared content

  • Anything you post in a band is visible to every member of that band.
  • Practice times and addresses are private to the band and cannot be made public. Only gigs can be announced.
  • If you leave a band you lose access to its future schedule and private recordings.
  • Don't add a rehearsal address you don't have permission to share.

8. Advertising

  • Jammy is free for musicians. Venues and music businesses pay for clearly labelled placements.
  • Every placement is reviewed by a person before it runs. We decline anything misleading, predatory, or aimed at taking money from musicians under false pretences.
  • Advertisers never receive your personal information.
  • An ad appearing on Jammy is not an endorsement. Use the same judgement you would anywhere else.

9. What Jammy does not promise

  • That you will find a band, a player, or a gig.
  • That other users are who they claim to be, or are any good.
  • That the service will be uninterrupted or error-free.
  • That content is stored forever. Keep your own copies of your recordings.

Jammy is provided "as is". To the fullest extent the law allows, we exclude implied warranties, and our total liability to you is limited to the amount you have paid us, which for a musician is zero. 10. Ending it

  • You can delete your account at any time from inside the app. It is permanent.
  • We can suspend or end your access if you break these terms.
  • If we ever shut Jammy down, we will give notice and a way to export your content.

We may update these terms. If a change materially affects you, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect. Continuing to use Jammy after that means you accept the new terms.
